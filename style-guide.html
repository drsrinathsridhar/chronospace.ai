Spacing, radius & shadows

Shadows

There are none. No box-shadow, no blur, no glassmorphism — every panel in the design file carries an empty effects array. Separation is done with a hairline instead:

c-blue-900#30323E · Card borders, dividers, section rules
c-blue-300#777CAD · Viewport-frame border
c-white-16p#FFFFFF29 · Navbar bottom edge

Layout & motion

A 1416px container on a 12-column grid with 20px gutters, and one easing curve for everything that moves.

Grid — 12 columns, 20px gutter

4 columns below 768px, 12 from there up. Page margins sit outside the container: 16px on mobile, 40px from md.
